## Objective

On `/board`, a visitor picks a school year and sees that year's board as a grid
of cards with name, role and photo, including people who have no photo on file.
An alum can send a friend `/board?term=2024-2025` and it opens on their year.

## Background

W42.2.1 made the data hold multiple terms, filtered the home page to the
current one, and created a bare `/board` page. This spec gives that page its
real layout.

The home board section (W38.3.2, W38.3.3) is playful: scattered rotation, GSAP
inertia on hover, a detail overlay. That suits a showcase of the people you're
about to meet. An archive is different: it's for looking someone up, and it may
grow to ten years of boards. It should feel like the same site but calmer: same
brand, same role-first card idea, no inertia.

Past members are also the ones most likely to have **no usable photo**. A grid
of broken-image icons would undercut the whole page.

## Specification

1. **Read the term from the URL.** `/board` with no parameter shows the current
   term. `/board?term=2024-2025` shows that term. A term that doesn't exist in
   the data shows the current term plus a short, polite note ("We don't have the
   2019–2020 board yet").

2. **Build `TermSwitcher`.** It lists every term from the W42.2.1 helper,
   newest first, and changes the URL when a term is chosen. A native `<select>`
   with a visible `<label>` is a perfectly good answer. If you build a row of
   buttons instead, it must expose which term is selected (`aria-pressed` or
   `aria-current`) and handle ten-plus terms at 375px. Say which you chose and
   why.

3. **Show a heading that names the term.** "2025–2026 Board" (or similar), and
   mark the current term as current ("Current board"). When the term changes,
   tell assistive tech: either move focus to that heading, or announce it in a
   polite live region. Not both.

4. **Build `ArchiveCard`.** Photo, full name and role, with role readable at a
   glance (W38.3.2's rule). Show major if the data has it. Use the brand
   tokens and the same font weights as the rest of the site. No GSAP inertia and
   no hover-only information: everything on an archive card is visible without
   interaction.

5. **Handle a missing photo.** Show a designed placeholder instead of a broken
   image: the person's initials on a brand-colored tile, optionally with an
   ornament from the Thai ornament kit. It must look intentional next to real
   photos. The initials tile is decorative (`aria-hidden`); the name is already
   in the card. Also handle a photo filename that 404s at runtime the same way.

6. **Alt text.** Real photos get alt text like "Alison Wang, President" (same
   rule as W38.3.2). No `alt=""` on photos of people.

7. **Order the cards on purpose.** President first is what visitors expect. Decide
   where order comes from (a role ranking in the component, or the order of the
   data) and say why in the PR.

8. **Empty or single-term state.** If the only term in the data is the current
   one, the switcher shouldn't be a dead control with one option. Hide it, and
   show a line inviting alumni to get in touch (link to the club email from
   W38.6).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `/board?term=…` opens on that term; a missing parameter shows the current term; an unknown term shows the current term with a note
- [ ] Choosing a term updates the URL; refresh and back/forward keep it in sync
- [ ] The current term is labeled as current
- [ ] Term change is announced once to a screen reader (VoiceOver or NVDA; write down what you heard)
- [ ] Switcher is fully keyboard-operable with a visible focus ring, and has an accessible name
- [ ] Every card shows name and role without interaction; major when present
- [ ] A member with no photo, and a member whose photo file 404s, both show the initials placeholder, never a broken image (screenshot both)
- [ ] Every real photo has descriptive alt text
- [ ] Card order rule explained in the PR
- [ ] With only one term, the switcher is hidden and an alumni invitation is shown
- [ ] Grid works at 375px with ten-plus cards and a long role ("Graphics Coordinator"); screenshot
- [ ] No brand hex

## Hints

- react-router's `useSearchParams` gives you the term and a setter. Using the
  setter adds a history entry, which is what makes the back button work.
- `onError` on an `<img>` fires when the file 404s. Switching to the placeholder
  from there covers the "filename typo" case too.
- Initials: `"Alison Wang"` → `"AW"`. Think about one-word names and names
  with three parts before you decide on the rule.
- If you move focus to the heading, the heading needs `tabIndex={-1}` to accept
  focus without becoming a Tab stop.
- A `<select>` with a `<label>` is announced correctly by every screen reader
  with no ARIA at all. That's a strong argument for it.
