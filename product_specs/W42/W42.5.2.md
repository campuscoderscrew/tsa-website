## Objective

Someone using only a keyboard or a screen reader can open the slide-out menu,
move through it, pick a destination and close it, and always know where focus
is. This week the menu becomes the way to reach three new pages, so it has to
work for everyone.

## Background

`StaggeredMenu` (about 610 lines, `src/components/staggered-menu/`) is the
site's main navigation and its most complex component. `features_TODO.md`
lists it twice:

- Accessibility audit: *"The `StaggeredMenu` slide-out: focus trap, Esc to
  close, `aria-expanded`."*
- Mobile navigation polish: *"Needs a review pass for touch targets, focus
  management, and behavior when opened mid-scroll."*

It came from a template and animates with GSAP. Until now it mostly linked to
sections on the home page. This week W42.1.1 adds **Events** and W42.4.1 adds
**Join** to `menuItems`, so the menu becomes the main way to reach other routes.

A slide-out menu that covers the page behaves like a modal. Common failures:

- the toggle doesn't say whether the menu is open
- Tab walks out of the menu into the page hidden behind it
- Esc does nothing
- closing the menu leaves focus on a link that's now off screen
- the page behind scrolls when you swipe the menu on a phone
- choosing an in-page section closes the menu but focus stays in the menu

W41.4.1 solved a similar problem for the gallery lightbox with native
`<dialog>`. That may or may not fit here: the menu's GSAP animation needs the
panel to exist while it slides. Find out before you choose.

## Specification

1. **Read the component first.** Before changing anything, write a short list in
   the PR of how it works today: what element the toggle is, how the panel is
   hidden when closed, where the GSAP timeline runs, and how items render
   (plain `<a href>` or router links). This list is the most useful part of the
   PR for the next person.

2. **The toggle.** It's a real `<button>` with an accessible name, and
   `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that reflect the panel. Its visible
   label or icon state changes with it.

3. **While closed,** nothing inside the panel can be focused or read by a screen
   reader.

4. **While open,**
   - focus moves to the first item in the panel
   - Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the panel and the toggle; the page behind
     can't be reached
   - the page behind doesn't scroll
   - Esc closes it

   Choose how: native `<dialog>`, the `inert` attribute on the rest of the page,
   or a focus trap. Say which and why in the PR.

5. **On close,** focus returns to the toggle. If the menu closed because an
   item was chosen, focus goes to the destination instead: the target section
   for an in-page link, or `#main-content` for a route (W42.5.1's route focus may
   already do this; don't fight it).

6. **Opened mid-scroll.** Open the menu halfway down the page, close it, and the
   page is still where it was. No jump to the top, no shift from the scrollbar
   disappearing.

7. **Touch targets.** Every item, the toggle and any social links in the panel
   are at least 44×44px at 375px.

8. **Reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the panel
   appears and disappears without the stagger or slide (an opacity fade is
   fine). Focus behavior must be identical with and without motion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] "How it works today" list is in the PR
- [ ] Toggle is a `<button>` with an accessible name, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that stay in sync
- [ ] Closed panel: nothing focusable or announced inside it
- [ ] Open panel: focus starts on the first item; Tab can't leave the panel; Esc closes it
- [ ] On close, focus returns to the toggle; after choosing an item, focus lands on the destination
- [ ] Page doesn't scroll behind the open menu, and keeps its position after closing (opened mid-page; recording in the PR)
- [ ] All menu targets are ≥ 44×44px at 375px; screenshot
- [ ] Reduced motion: no stagger or slide; focus behaves the same
- [ ] **Screen reader** (VoiceOver or NVDA): opening announces the menu and its state; write down what you heard
- [ ] Works with the new Events and Join items, if merged; otherwise tested with a temporary extra item that's removed before commit
- [ ] No brand hex (the GSAP color props may keep using `TSA_COLORS`, as agreed in W41)

## Hints

- `inert` on a container makes everything inside it unfocusable and hidden from
  assistive tech in one attribute. Check which element would need it: the
  menu probably renders inside the page it would make inert.
- `showModal()` puts a `<dialog>` in the browser's top layer and makes the rest
  of the page inert for you, but it opens immediately. Read how others animate
  a `<dialog>` closing before you commit to it.
- Locking scroll by setting `overflow: hidden` on `<body>` removes the scrollbar
  and shifts the layout sideways. `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on `<html>` is one
  fix.
- GSAP's `gsap.matchMedia()` can branch on `(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
  The About section already uses it. Read that first.
- Test with the keyboard before the screen reader. Most bugs show up just by
  pressing Tab and watching where the focus ring goes.
