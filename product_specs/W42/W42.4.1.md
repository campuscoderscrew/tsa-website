## Objective

A prospective member lands on `/join` and learns, in one short page, what TSA
is, what a GBM is, how to get involved, and what to do next. Officers get one
link to put in their Instagram bio or on a flyer.

## Background

The footer says "we're always welcoming new members." W38.6 pointed that
sentence at something real (an email, Instagram, or the Linktree), but
`features_TODO.md` notes that a proper join flow is "a feature of its own":
*what a GBM is, when they meet, what dues cost if any, a form.*

Most visitors arrive from TSA's Instagram on a phone, often as a first-year who
has never been to a GBM and isn't Thai. The question in their head is "can I
just show up?" Right now nothing on the site answers it.

Some of the answers are things only the club knows (`features_TODO.md`, open
question 4: "Is there a regular meeting location and time to publish?"). The
page must be useful even if those answers don't arrive this week, and easy to
update when they do.

This feature has three parts:

- **W42.4.1 (this spec):** the page, its content structure, and links to it.
- **W42.4.2:** an interest form or sign-up step.
- **W42.4.3:** an FAQ.

## Specification

1. **Ask the club first.** Message Alison Wang (awang527@terpmail.umd.edu):
   is there a regular GBM time and place, are there dues, does someone need to be
   Thai or speak Thai to join (the answer is almost certainly no, but it should
   come from them), and what's the single next step they want a new person to
   take. Paste the message in the PR.

2. **Put the content in `src/content/join.ts`.** Typed data for: a short
   intro, a "What's a GBM?" explainer, a "How to get involved" list of steps,
   and meeting details (time, place, dues) as **optional** fields. Comment at
   the top on how a non-developer edits it.

3. **Never publish unconfirmed logistics.** If a meeting time, location or dues
   amount isn't confirmed, leave the field unset. The page shows a fallback
   instead ("GBM dates are posted on @umdtsa"), not a guess. Mark each unset
   field with `// TODO: awaiting club` and list them in the PR.

4. **Build `JoinPage`.** Follow this week's page conventions (one `<h1>`,
   `<main id="main-content" tabIndex={-1}>`, title
   `Join · Thai Student Association at UMD`) and the brand header pattern.
   Lead with a welcoming line that answers "can I just show up?", then the
   sections from step 2, then **one** clear call to action. Until W42.4.2 lands,
   the CTA uses the next step the club named (or the W38.6 contact link).

5. **Register the route and link to it.** One `<Route>` line in `App.tsx`
   (Track E's file; post first). A "Join" item in the slide-out menu
   (`menuItems` in `HomePage.tsx`). Point the footer's join CTA at `/join`.
   Internal links go through the router or the base path (see this week's
   README).

6. **Make it shareable.** `/tsa-website/join` must load directly and on hard
   refresh. Officers will paste it into an Instagram bio, so test the exact URL
   you'd give them.

7. **Keep it short.** One screen of reading on a laptop, two or three on a
   phone. If a section gets long, it belongs in the FAQ (W42.4.3).

## Hints

- Before you add the menu item, check how `StaggeredMenu` renders links. If
  it's a plain `<a href>`, a route needs the base path in front of it.
- Optional fields in TypeScript (`time?: string`) plus a conditional render is
  all the "fallback" logic needs.
- Write the intro for a nervous first-year. Short sentences. "You don't need to
  be Thai" (if the club confirms it) does more work than a paragraph about the
  club's mission.
- A paragraph of text on navy or crimson needs the same AA check as anything
  else. Body text is 4.5:1.
