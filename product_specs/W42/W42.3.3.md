## Objective

The home page shows the student organizations TSA regularly works with, each
linking to that organization's own page. A visitor sees that TSA is part of a
wider campus community, and partner clubs get a little visibility in return.

## Background

TSA collaborates constantly. Their event posts on Instagram feature joint events
with **NSA, MCA, KSA and VSA**, among others. None of that shows on the site.
`features_TODO.md` lists a partners strip under "Nice to have": it "would show
the club is plugged into a wider community."

Two things make this less simple than it looks:

- **We don't know the full names.** Acronyms like "NSA" or "MCA" can stand for
  several different campus organizations. Guessing the expansion and linking to
  the wrong club is worse than showing nothing.
- **Logos belong to other clubs.** Using another organization's logo needs that
  organization's permission. A strip of well-set text names, in TSA's type and
  colors, looks intentional and needs nobody's permission.

## Specification

1. **Get the list from the club.** Ask Alison Wang (awang527@terpmail.umd.edu)
   which organizations TSA wants to list, their full names, and the link each
   would like used (usually their Instagram). Paste the message in the PR.
   Until you hear back, include only organizations whose full name and account
   you can confirm from TSA's own posts tagging them. Don't expand an acronym
   by guessing.

2. **Create `src/data/partners.ts`.** A typed list with, per organization: a
   stable `id`, the full `name`, a `shortName` (the acronym), and a `url`. Add
   an optional `logo` field only if you have written permission for at least one
   logo; otherwise leave logos out entirely. Comment at the top: how to add a
   partner, and that logos need the other club's permission.

3. **Build `PartnerStrip`.** A section with a heading ("Friends of TSA",
   "Our partners", or the club's preference), using the site's eyebrow/heading
   pattern at a smaller scale than a main section. Each organization appears as
   a chip or wordmark showing the short name, with the full name visible or
   available to assistive tech. Each one links to its `url`, opening in a new
   tab with `rel="noreferrer"` like the footer links (W38.6).

4. **Place it.** Pick where on the home page it goes (after Events, before the
   footer, or near the board section) and add one line to `HomePage.tsx`. That's
   Track D's file; post in the group chat before you push. Say why you chose that
   spot in the PR.

5. **Restraint.** The brief is "colorful but not overwhelming." This is a
   supporting strip, not a hero. No marquee or auto-scrolling ticker (it can't
   be paused and fails reduced motion). A static wrap of chips is the right
   default.

6. **Hide it when empty.** If the list ends up empty because nothing was
   confirmed, the section renders nothing, not a heading with no content.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every organization listed has a confirmed full name and link; the message to the club is in the PR
- [ ] No acronym expansion was guessed; anything awaiting confirmation is left out and listed in the PR
- [ ] No partner logo is used without written permission (and if any is, the permission is noted in the PR)
- [ ] Each link opens the partner's page in a new tab with `rel="noreferrer"`; every link clicked and checked
- [ ] Each link's accessible name includes the full organization name (check with VoiceOver or NVDA)
- [ ] Keyboard: all chips are reachable with Tab and have a visible focus ring
- [ ] Chips wrap cleanly at 375px with no horizontal scroll; tap targets at least 44px tall; screenshot
- [ ] Nothing animates or auto-scrolls
- [ ] An empty list renders nothing
- [ ] No brand hex

## Out of Scope

- Partner logos you don't have permission for
- A dedicated partners page
- Showing which events were collaborations (that's event data; note it in `features_TODO.md` if the club wants it)
- Changing other home sections

## Hints

- `aria-label` on a link replaces its visible text for screen readers. If the
  chip shows "KSA" and the label says the full name, make sure the visible text
  is contained in the label, or voice-control users ("click KSA") can't
  activate it. Look up WCAG "Label in Name."
- A wrapping list (`<ul>` with flex-wrap) tells a screen reader how many
  partners there are for free.
- If you're tempted by a scrolling marquee: WCAG 2.2.2 requires a pause control
  for anything that moves for more than five seconds.
