## Objective

The `/join` page answers the questions new members actually ask ("Do I need to
be Thai?", "Is there a cost?", "Can I come to just one event?") in a short,
expandable FAQ. An officer replying to a DM can link straight to one answer.

## Background

W42.4.1 kept the join page deliberately short and said that anything long
belongs in an FAQ. New members' questions come up again and again in DMs, and
each one the site answers is one fewer reply an officer has to type.

An expandable FAQ is a classic **disclosure** (show/hide) pattern. It's easy to
get wrong:

- the question is a `<div>` with a click handler, so a keyboard can't open it
- the expanded state isn't exposed, so a screen reader doesn't know it opened
- answers are removed from the page when closed, so browser find (Ctrl+F)
  can't find them
- the whole FAQ animates open with a height transition that ignores reduced
  motion

HTML has a native disclosure element (`<details>` / `<summary>`) that solves
most of this. The other approach is a `<button>` with `aria-expanded`
controlling a region. Both are acceptable; you should know what each gives you.

## Specification

1. **Collect real questions.** Ask the club which questions they get most from
   new members, and ask for (or draft for their approval) the answers. Five to
   eight questions is plenty. Paste the exchange in the PR. Anything the club
   hasn't approved carries `// TODO: awaiting club` and is listed in the PR.

2. **Add the entries to `src/content/join.ts`.** Each one has a stable `id`
   (a short slug like `do-i-need-to-be-thai`), a question and an answer. The
   `id` becomes the link anchor, so it must never change once published;
   say that in a comment.

3. **Build `JoinFaq`.** Choose `<details>`/`<summary>` or a button with
   `aria-expanded`, and say why in the PR. Requirements either way:
   - each question is reachable with Tab and toggles with Enter and Space
   - the open/closed state is exposed to assistive tech
   - a visible indicator (a chevron or plus) shows the state; it's decorative
   - all answers are closed by default, and opening one doesn't close the others

4. **Linkable answers.** `/join#do-i-need-to-be-thai` opens the page with that
   answer **expanded** and scrolled into view, and moves focus to its question.
   Add a small "copy link" or "link to this answer" affordance only if it fits
   cleanly; the anchor working is the requirement, the button is a bonus.

5. **Motion.** If opening animates, it's a short transition, and it's
   instant under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

6. **Placement.** The FAQ goes below the main join content and above the
   footer, with an `<h2>` ("Questions") that fits the page's heading order.

## Hints

- MDN's `<details>` page covers the `toggle` event and the `open` attribute.
  Note how browser find-in-page behaves with closed `<details>` in different
  browsers.
- The router may handle hash scrolling for you, or may not. Check what
  happens on a direct load versus a client-side navigation to `/join#…`; they
  often behave differently.
- If you build the button version, read the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices
  "Disclosure (Show/Hide)" pattern; don't use the "Accordion" pattern's
  arrow-key navigation unless you've decided you need it.
- Animating `height: auto` is hard in CSS. A grid-rows trick
  (`grid-template-rows: 0fr` → `1fr`) is one approach; skipping the animation
  is another perfectly good one.
