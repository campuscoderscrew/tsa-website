## Objective

Move events out of the component and into a typed data file that uses real
dates. The "Upcoming Events" section should show events that are actually
upcoming. This also gives the calendar TSA asked for a data shape to build on.

## Background

TSA's intake form asked for **"a calendar"** and an **"archive of previous
events."** Neither can exist until the site knows what an event *is*. Today
`UpcomingEvents.tsx` hardcodes three items with display strings, not dates:

```
Water Festival   "APR 21"   "Campus green"
Loy Krathong     "MAY 03"   "Campus pond"
Trivia Night     "JUN 14"   "Student union"
```

All three venues are generic placeholders. **Loy Krathong in May is wrong**:
it's held on the full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month, which lands in
November. A Thai student would notice that immediately. Because the dates are
strings with no year, the site can't tell what's past and what's upcoming. So
"Upcoming Events" shows April events in October.

Real data exists, but it's partial. In April, a branch nobody merged
(`feature/upcoming-events-images`) replaced the placeholders with real TSA
events and photos:

```
Trivia Night      "MAR 12"   Tawes 1107        public/trivia_night.jpg
Water Festival    "APR 18"   Hornbake Plaza    public/water_festival.jpg
Senior Send-Off   "DEC 3"    Tawes 1100        public/senior_send.jpg
```

That branch is 16 commits behind and uses `src="/trivia_night.jpg"`, which
404s under `/tsa-website/`. Mine it for content; don't merge it.

**The big open question is still open:** will the club keep events in this
repo, or in a Google Calendar the site reads? (`features_TODO.md`, open
question 1.) This spec doesn't decide that. It designs a data shape that works
for both. Today a developer edits `events.ts`. Later, a Google Calendar fetch
could produce the same array.

## Specification

1. **Design the `TsaEvent` type** in `src/data/events.ts`. At minimum:
   - a stable `id`
   - `title`
   - `start` as an **ISO 8601 date or date-time string**. This must be a real
     date that can be sorted and compared, not a display string.
   - optional `end`
   - `venue`
   - a `category` from a small union you define (festival, GBM, social,
     fundraiser, collab — look at TSA's Instagram and decide)
   - optional `instagramUrl` (the post for that event)
   - `media`: keep the existing image/video union
   - optional `description`

   Before you finalize it, open the Google Calendar API's `Event` resource
   docs and check that every field you defined has an obvious source there
   (`summary`, `start.date` / `start.dateTime`, `location`, `description`, …).
   Put that mapping in a comment. It's what makes a later move to Calendar
   cheap.

2. **Fill it with real data.** Ask Alison Wang (awang527@terpmail.umd.edu) or
   the club's Instagram for this semester's events. Until you hear back:
   - Use the three events from the old branch. They're from the 2025–26
     school year, so they belong in **past** events. Trivia and Water Festival
     are most likely spring 2026. Senior Send-Off "DEC 3" is probably December
     2025. Confirm the years.
   - Mark each date that someone at TSA hasn't confirmed with a `// TODO: unconfirmed`
     comment, the same convention as W38.3.1.
   - **Don't invent upcoming events.** An empty "upcoming" list is better than
     a fake one (see step 4).
   - Fix Loy Krathong. If the club hasn't given you this year's date, leave it
     out rather than guess.

   Copy any images you use from the old branch with
   `git checkout origin/feature/upcoming-events-images -- public/<file>`,
   check their size, and reference them through `import.meta.env.BASE_URL`.

3. **Derive, don't store.** Export small helpers for the derived views:
   upcoming events (start ≥ today, soonest first) and past events (most recent
   first). The component formats the date for display (`NOV 14`) from the ISO
   string. No display strings in the data.

4. **Handle the edge cases in the carousel:**
   - **No upcoming events.** This happens every winter and summer break. Show
     a friendly empty state that points to @umdtsa on Instagram, instead of an
     empty or broken carousel.
   - **One or two upcoming events.** The fan is built for three or more.
     Decide what it does with fewer, and test it. Thanks to W41.3.1 the card
     count can now change safely.

5. **Keep the section heading honest.** If the carousel can show past events
   (because there are no upcoming ones and you chose to fall back), it must
   not say "Upcoming". Pick a behavior and explain it in the PR.

## Hints

- **The time zone trap:** `new Date("2026-11-14")` is parsed as **midnight
  UTC**. In College Park that's 7 or 8 PM on Nov 13. Format it in the browser's
  local zone and you get the wrong day. Look up how date-only ISO strings are
  parsed versus date-time strings, then pick an approach.
- `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with `{ month: "short", day: "numeric" }` gives you
  "Nov 14". Uppercasing is a styling choice; do it in CSS.
- "Today" in a static site means "when the visitor loads the page," not "when
  we built it." Make sure your comparison runs in the browser, not at build
  time.
