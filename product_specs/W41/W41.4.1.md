## Objective

Clicking a gallery photo opens it large and full-screen, with its caption.
The visitor can move between photos and close the viewer with a mouse, a
finger or the keyboard.

## Background

TSA asked for "a gallery for all events." W38.4.1 made the gallery look like
the rest of the site, and W38.4.2 gives every photo alt text, an event and a
date. But the photos are still thumbnails in a masonry grid that you can't
open. On a phone, a group photo at one-column width is too small to find
your friends in, and finding your friends is most of why people look at a club
gallery.

`features_TODO.md` lists the lightbox with a warning: *"must be keyboard-
navigable and must trap focus correctly — this is a genuinely easy thing to
build badly."* Common failures:

- focus stays on the page behind the viewer, so Tab walks through hidden links
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> does nothing
- closing it drops focus at the top of the page instead of the photo you opened
- the page behind scrolls when you swipe on the image
- a screen reader isn't told a dialog opened

The browser's native `<dialog>` element, opened with `showModal()`, solves
most of these for free. Find out which ones before you decide whether you
need anything else.

## Specification

1. **Make tiles openable.** Each gallery tile becomes (or contains) a real
   `<button>`. Its accessible name comes from the photo's alt text or caption.
   Hover and focus styles match the site. The tile must look the same as
   before at rest.

2. **Build `Lightbox`** as its own component that takes the list of items and
   the open index, plus callbacks. Inside it:
   - the photo, as large as fits the viewport (`object-contain`), on a dark
     navy scrim from the tokens
   - the caption, event and date from the data, plus a position counter ("4 of 9")
   - Close, Previous and Next buttons with accessible names, ≥ 44px
   - <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes; <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> navigate
   - wrap around at the ends, or disable the buttons there. Pick one.

3. **Focus management.**
   - On open, focus moves into the dialog.
   - While it's open, Tab can't reach anything behind it.
   - On close, focus returns to **the tile that opened it**, even if the user
     navigated to a different photo meanwhile. Decide whether focus should go
     to the original tile or the photo they ended on, and justify the choice.

4. **Scroll lock.** The page behind doesn't scroll while the lightbox is open,
   and the scroll position is unchanged after it closes.

5. **Touch.** A horizontal swipe on the image goes to the next or previous
   photo. A tap on the scrim outside the image closes it. Be careful that the
   tap that *ends* a swipe doesn't count as a scrim tap.

6. **Video posters.** Three gallery items are frames from event videos. Use
   whatever W38.4.2 decided for them (marked, or noted for follow-up). Don't
   embed video players in the lightbox in this spec.

7. **Motion.** A short fade or scale on open/close is welcome. Under
   `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it's instant.

8. **Performance.** Thumbnails keep `loading="lazy"`. When the lightbox shows
   photo *n*, preload *n+1* and *n−1* so arrowing through feels instant. Don't
   preload the whole gallery.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every tile opens the lightbox with mouse, tap, and keyboard (Tab + Enter)
- [ ] <kbd>Esc</kbd>, the Close button, and a scrim tap all close it
- [ ] ← / → and the on-screen buttons move between photos; behavior at the ends is deliberate
- [ ] Tab never reaches page content while open (tabbed through all of it; written up in the PR)
- [ ] Focus returns to the right tile on close (justification in the PR)
- [ ] Background doesn't scroll; scroll position preserved after close
- [ ] Swipe works on a real phone; a swipe never closes the viewer by accident
- [ ] **Screen reader:** opening announces a dialog and the photo's description; moving to the next photo announces it. Note what you heard (VoiceOver or NVDA).
- [ ] Neighbors preload (check the network tab); the grid still lazy-loads
- [ ] Reduced motion respected
- [ ] Colors are tokens; no brand hex
- [ ] At 375px the photo, caption and controls fit without overlapping; screenshot

## Out of Scope

- Deep-linking to a photo (`/gallery?photo=4`). It's tempting, but it touches routing and `GalleryTransitionProvider`. Add it to `features_TODO.md` if you want it.
- Albums / multiple events per page
- Pinch-zoom inside the lightbox (the browser's own zoom is enough for now)
- Playing videos

## Hints

- MDN's `<dialog>` page, the section on `showModal()`. Note what it does to
  the rest of the page (the "inert" behavior) and what it does with
  <kbd>Esc</kbd>. If you use it, listen for the `close` and `cancel` events
  instead of reimplementing Esc.
- React + `<dialog>`: you'll call `showModal()` / `close()` from an effect,
  using a ref. Don't control it with the `open` attribute; that opens it
  *non-modally*.
- `::backdrop` styles the scrim of a modal `<dialog>`.
- Store the trigger element (or its index) when opening, so you can restore
  focus on close.
- `touch-action` on the image controls whether the browser or your code gets
  the horizontal swipe.
