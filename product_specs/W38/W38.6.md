## Objective

Replace the placeholder contact information and dead social links with TSA's real
details, so a prospective member who scrolls to the bottom can actually reach the
club.

## Background

This is the cheapest high-impact fix on the board. The footer is the last thing a
visitor sees and the only place on the site with any contact information — and
almost none of it is real.

What's wrong, concretely:

| What it says | Reality |
| --- | --- |
| `hello@tsa.club` | Not TSA's address. Theirs is **umdtsa@gmail.com**. |
| "Google Maps" link with `href="#"` | Goes nowhere. |
| "on campus / thai student association" | Says nothing. |
| LinkedIn icon → `linkedin.com` | The site root. TSA has no LinkedIn presence we know of. |
| Instagram icon → `instagram.com` | The site root. Theirs is **instagram.com/umdtsa**. |
| TikTok icon → `tiktok.com` | The site root. |
| "*we reply fastest on socials." | True, and the socials don't work. |

The nav menu in `HomePage.tsx` has the same problem: `socialItems` points at
`https://instagram.com` and `https://facebook.com`.

TSA explicitly asked for **"Links to Other Platforms."** Their Instagram bio
points to **linktr.ee/umcptsa**, which is their actual hub.

Known-good details, from TSA's own materials:

- Email: `umdtsa@gmail.com`
- Instagram: `https://www.instagram.com/umdtsa/`
- Linktree: `https://linktr.ee/umcptsa`
- Org: Thai Student Association, University of Maryland, College Park
- Contact person on file: Alison Wang (`awang527@terpmail.umd.edu`)

**Confirm the rest with the club before publishing.** Do not guess at a TikTok
handle or invent a meeting location.

## Specification

1. **Ask the club first.** Before writing code, get from a TSA officer: the
   correct email, every social account they actually use (with handles), whether
   they have a physical meeting spot, and whether they want a Linktree link. This
   is a ten-minute Discord message and it's step one, not an afterthought.

   If you can't reach them this week, implement the known-good values above,
   remove the ones you can't verify, and list the open questions in your PR.

2. **Fix the email.** `umdtsa@gmail.com`, as a working `mailto:` link.

3. **Only link socials that exist.** Remove the icon entirely rather than
   pointing it at a platform's homepage. Three icons that work beat five where
   two are dead — a dead link reads as an abandoned club, which is the opposite
   of the impression TSA wants.

   At minimum Instagram must be correct, since it's their primary channel.

4. **Add the Linktree link.** It's their existing hub for everything else and it
   directly answers the "Links to Other Platforms" request. Give it a clear label
   rather than only an icon.

5. **Fix or remove the Google Maps link.** `href="#"` is worse than nothing — it
   looks clickable and does nothing. If TSA has a regular meeting location, link
   a real Maps URL. If not, remove the link and replace the vague "on campus /
   thai student association" block with something true: University of Maryland,
   College Park.

6. **Fix the nav menu socials** in `HomePage.tsx`. Same rule: real URLs or gone.
   Keep it consistent with the footer — don't have Facebook in the menu and not
   in the footer.

7. **Make the join CTA actionable.** "we're always welcoming new members" is a
   nice sentence with nothing to click. Point it at whatever the real next step
   is — emailing the club, the Instagram, the Linktree, a GBM schedule. One
   concrete action.

8. **Add `rel="noreferrer"` and `target="_blank"` on external links.** The
   existing social links already do this correctly — match the pattern on
   anything new. Keep the `aria-label`s too; they're right.

9. **Leave the layout alone.** This is a content and correctness pass. The
   three-column grid, the big lowercase "tsa" wordmark, and the pill components
   all stay. If something you fix genuinely needs a layout change, keep it minimal
   and say so in the PR.

## Hints

- Click every link after you're done. Every one. This spec's entire value is that
  the links work, and the failure mode — a typo'd handle that 404s — is invisible
  from the code.
- A `mailto:` can prefill a subject: `mailto:umdtsa@gmail.com?subject=Interested%20in%20joining%20TSA`.
  Small touch, lowers the barrier for a nervous first-year.
- Instagram URLs work with or without `www` and a trailing slash. Use the
  canonical form from the browser address bar.
- If you remove an icon, remove its whole `<a>` block including the inline SVG —
  don't leave dead markup behind.
- The SVG icons are inline paths with `fill="currentColor"`, so they inherit
  color correctly. If you add a Linktree icon, match that pattern.
- Ask the club whether they want their Gmail public. They listed it on a public
  form, so it's very likely fine — but asking costs nothing and it's their
  address, not ours.
